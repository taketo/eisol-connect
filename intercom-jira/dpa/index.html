Data Processing Addendum — EISOL Support Sync

Last updated: 2026-11-28

This Data Processing Addendum ("DPA") is between EISOL LLC (英智ソリューションズ合同会社), 2-5-1 Sekimae, Musashino-shi, Tokyo 180-0014, Japan ("EISOL", "we") and the organisation that installs EISOL Support Sync ("the app") on its Jira Cloud site ("the customer", "you"). It forms part of the end-user agreement for the app (the Bonterms Standard End User Agreement, Version 1.0, whose Provider-Specific Terms for the app identify this DPA) and applies from the time the app is installed. If you need a signed copy, email support@eisol.jp.

1. Roles

  • You are the controller of the personal data in the content the app syncs and in the Intercom data the app handles. You decide which Intercom workspace is connected and what is copied, by the app's settings, and which conversations are linked to which issues, by creating issues with the data connector and linking conversations in the panel next to an issue.
  • EISOL is your processor for the processing the app performs on your behalf. The app performs the configured processing on Atlassian Forge and through your Jira site and Intercom workspace. EISOL is responsible for that app processing; Forge hosting does not transfer that responsibility to Atlassian. The privacy policy separately describes Marketplace reporting, shared operational logs and support correspondence received by EISOL.
  • Annex 1 describes the processing. Words such as "personal data", "processing", "controller", "processor" and "personal data breach" have the meanings given in the GDPR and the UK GDPR.

2. Our commitments

  1. Instructions. The app processes personal data only as set by your settings and by the links your users make between conversations and issues, which are your documented instructions, unless the law requires otherwise; in that case we tell you first, unless the law forbids it. If we believe an instruction breaks data protection law, we tell you.
  2. Confidentiality. Anyone at EISOL who works on the app is bound to keep personal data confidential.
  3. Security. We maintain the measures in Annex 2.
  4. Sub-processors. You authorise the sub-processors in Annex 3. We will announce any new sub-processor on the app's documentation page at least 30 days before it starts. You may object in writing within that period; if we cannot address the objection, you may stop using the app.
  5. Your Intercom workspace. The app reads data from, and writes data to, the Intercom workspace you connect. Intercom processes that data under your own agreement with Intercom, and is not our sub-processor.
  6. Requests from individuals. Most requests can be met directly in your Jira site and Intercom workspace. If you need more help, we assist you by email. If an individual contacts us directly, we pass the request to you.
  7. Support requests. If you send us personal data from your synced content in a support request, we use it only to answer that request, with the support sub-processors in Annex 3, and we delete it with the support correspondence, three years after the last message.
  8. Assistance. We give you the information you reasonably need for security, breach notification, data protection impact assessments and consultation with authorities, as far as it concerns the app.
  9. Personal data breaches. We notify you without undue delay, and within 48 hours of becoming aware of a breach affecting your personal data, with the information we have at the time, and we update you as we learn more.
  10. Deletion. After actual uninstallation, Atlassian manages the app's stored data under its Forge storage lifecycle, with a currently documented 28-day retention/recovery window. Reinstalling alone does not restore prior data. Copies soft-deleted by app storage APIs and backups remain subject to Atlassian's retention rules; deletion is not a promise of immediate erasure from every backup. An administrator can also delete the app's data at any time on the settings page: Disconnect deletes the app's Intercom token, and Use another account then deletes the workspace's details, the data connector's secret and record, the settings, links, sync positions, waiting changes, recent issues and error messages. The app does not revoke its token in Intercom, because the same workspace may be connected to other Jira sites, so the app's access to your workspace lasts until the app's token is revoked in Intercom (Settings → API tokens → Revoke); uninstalling the app from Jira does not revoke it. Issues and comments the app wrote to Jira, and the internal notes and the data connector "Create Jira issue" it wrote to your Intercom workspace, remain until separately removed; Disconnect, Use another account and uninstalling do not delete them. The app also limits what it keeps: recent issues keep the last ten entries and error messages the last 30; a Jira change that could not be written to Intercom is tried again for a day and then dropped, and if it cannot be tried in that time, Forge deletes it two days after its last try; an approval that waits for its confirmation code expires after 10 minutes, and Forge then deletes it. The app keeps no Atlassian account IDs.
  11. Demonstrating compliance. We make available the information needed to show that we meet this DPA: this DPA, the privacy policy, and written answers to reasonable security questionnaires. If an audit or inspection beyond that is required by law, you may carry it out, or have an independent auditor carry it out, at your own cost, on at least 30 days' written notice, no more than once a year, without access to other customers' data.
  12. California. Where the California Consumer Privacy Act of 2018, as amended, and its regulations ("CCPA") apply to personal information the app processes for you, EISOL acts as your service provider. We process that personal information only to provide the app to you, as set out in this DPA and the end-user agreement. We do not sell or share it, and we do not retain, use or disclose it for any other purpose or outside our direct business relationship with you. We do not combine it with personal information we receive from others or collect ourselves, except as the CCPA permits. We comply with the CCPA's obligations for service providers and provide the same level of privacy protection the CCPA requires. If we can no longer meet these obligations, we tell you, and you may take reasonable and appropriate steps to stop and remediate unauthorised use. We certify that we understand and will comply with these restrictions.

3. Transfers

EISOL is established in Japan, which the European Commission has recognised as providing an adequate level of protection for personal data (Commission Implementing Decision (EU) 2019/419). The app's processing takes place on Atlassian's platform, in your Jira site and in your Intercom workspace, under the terms of those services. EISOL also accesses Marketplace license/contact reports in Japan and can receive shared operational logs and support correspondence, as described in the privacy policy. This DPA covers processing performed by the app on your behalf; it does not describe all of EISOL's separate business records. The provider facts below and the privacy policy distinguish hosting facts from unresolved workspace-specific and Pages-log disclosures. The privacy policy also describes EISOL's own records: where support email is kept, the AI services EISOL uses to translate and draft support replies, and how long EISOL keeps support and accounting records. No app-wide EEA-only or PINNED assurance is given.

Intercom hosts each workspace in the United States, the European Union or Australia, depending on the workspace (Intercom's regional data hosting). Your agreement with Intercom applies to that data. These are provider-wide facts, not a confirmation of where your workspace's data is kept.

4. General

  • If this DPA and the end-user agreement conflict on the protection of personal data, this DPA prevails.
  • Each party's liability under this DPA is subject to the limitations in the end-user agreement, except where the law does not allow them.
  • This DPA lasts as long as the app processes your personal data. We may update it to reflect changes in the law or in the app; changes that reduce your protection apply only with 30 days' notice on the app's documentation page.

Annex 2 — Security measures

  • The sync code runs on Atlassian Forge and sends conversation content, Jira comments and status changes between Jira and the connected Intercom workspace. EISOL operates no separate sync backend. The current app has no support feature for exporting stored data or Intercom data to EISOL; this does not exclude the Marketplace, log and support information disclosed in the privacy policy.
  • The app connects to Intercom with OAuth and never sees Intercom passwords. It asks Intercom only for the permissions it needs: reading and writing conversations, reading admins, and reading and writing data connectors. The Intercom token is kept in Forge's encrypted secret storage, and the app's client secret in an encrypted Forge variable. No secret is kept in the source code.
  • The data connector calls the app with a random secret that the app sets in the connector and keeps in Forge's encrypted secret storage. The app creates the data connector for teammates only, with Fin's use turned off. The connector sends only the conversation's ID and receives only the issue's key and address. Customer authentication is off because nothing is returned to the customer, and Set it up again turns it off again.
  • A connection starts only when a Jira administrator of the site enters the confirmation code shown after the approval in Intercom.
  • All traffic uses HTTPS.
  • Only Jira administrators can change the app's settings, and only project administrators can link or unlink conversations; both are checked against the person's own Jira permissions. The panel next to an issue checks the access of the person using it.
  • Apart from reopening a closed or snoozed conversation when its issue is done (if that setting is on), everything the app writes to conversations is an internal note, which your customers do not see; the app sends no messages to your customers. Jira comments restricted to a role or group, and comments written by apps, are not written to Intercom.
  • Apart from Jira's answers in short error messages (up to 300 characters), the app does not store message text, the names or email addresses of customers or teammates, or teammates' IDs.
  • The app writes no message content, names or email addresses to its logs, only what was involved (issue keys, conversation and message IDs), counts, and the kind of error, including Intercom's error code. Site administrators can stop Atlassian from sharing the app's logs with EISOL.
  • The app's dependencies are checked for known vulnerabilities before each release, and vulnerabilities are fixed within the times set by Atlassian's security bug fix policy.
