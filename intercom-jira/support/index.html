Support and service levels — EISOL Support Sync

Last updated: 2026-11-28

How to get help

Raise a request in the EISOL support portal, or email support@eisol.jp, in English or Japanese. Please include your Jira site address and, if you can, the Jira issue key or the Intercom conversation number concerned.

Response times

RequestFirst response
Critical: the sync has stopped for all linked conversations, the data connector has stopped creating issues, or the app is writing wrong dataWithin 24 hours, every day
Everything else: questions, smaller faults, requestsWithin 5 business days

Business days are Monday to Friday in Japan, excluding Japanese public holidays. We also answer within 24 hours any request that Atlassian identifies as critical.

What support covers

  • Help with installing, connecting and setting up the app.
  • Investigating faults in the app, fixing them, and telling you when the fix is released.
  • Questions about how the app handles your data, including Intercom data (see the privacy policy).

It does not cover faults in Jira or Intercom themselves, or changes to your own configuration in those products, though we will tell you what we find.

Security

Report a security problem to support@eisol.jp with "Security" in the subject. We acknowledge it within 24 hours. We fix critical vulnerabilities within 10 days and high-severity ones within 4 weeks, in line with Atlassian's requirements for Marketplace apps.

How the app is run

The app runs on Atlassian Forge and depends on the availability and limits of Jira, Forge and Intercom. Jira comments and status changes normally reach Intercom at once, when Jira reports them. The app reads changes made in Intercom every five minutes. Large backlogs, rate limits and temporary failures can delay delivery across several runs. If Jira or Intercom is busy or cannot be reached, the app tries again: a message on the next run, and a note every five minutes for a day. Release notes are in the user guide.

If the app is withdrawn

If we ever stop offering the app, we will keep supporting existing customers for at least one year.
