Data Processing Addendum — EISOL Calendar Sync

Last updated: 2026-11-22

This Data Processing Addendum ("DPA") is between EISOL LLC (英智ソリューションズ合同会社), 2-5-1 Sekimae, Musashino-shi, Tokyo 180-0014, Japan ("EISOL", "we") and the organisation that installs EISOL Calendar Sync ("the app") on its Jira Cloud site ("the customer", "you"). It forms part of the end-user agreement for the app (the Bonterms Standard End User Agreement, Version 1.0, whose Provider-Specific Terms for the app identify this DPA) and applies from the time the app is installed. If you need a signed copy, email support@eisol.jp.

1. Roles

  • You are the controller of the personal data in the content the app syncs and in the Google account data the app handles. You decide which issues are copied to which calendars, by setting the app's rules, and which Google account is connected.
  • EISOL is your processor for the processing the app performs on your behalf. The app performs the configured processing on Atlassian Forge and through your Jira site and Google account. EISOL is responsible for that app processing; Forge hosting does not transfer that responsibility to Atlassian. The privacy policy separately describes Marketplace reporting, shared operational logs and support correspondence received by EISOL.
  • Annex 1 describes the processing. Words such as "personal data", "processing", "controller", "processor" and "personal data breach" have the meanings given in the GDPR and the UK GDPR.

2. Our commitments

  1. Instructions. The app processes personal data only as set by your rules and settings, which are your documented instructions, unless the law requires otherwise; in that case we tell you first, unless the law forbids it. If we believe an instruction breaks data protection law, we tell you.
  2. Confidentiality. Anyone at EISOL who works on the app is bound to keep personal data confidential.
  3. Security. We maintain the measures in Annex 2.
  4. Sub-processors. You authorise the sub-processors in Annex 3. We will announce any new sub-processor on the app's documentation page at least 30 days before it starts. You may object in writing within that period; if we cannot address the objection, you may stop using the app.
  5. Your Google account. The app writes data to the Google account you connect. Google processes that data under the terms that apply to that account, for example your organisation's Google Workspace agreement, and is not our sub-processor.
  6. Requests from individuals. Most requests can be met directly in your Jira site and Google account. If you need more help, we assist you by email. If an individual contacts us directly, we pass the request to you.
  7. Support requests. If you send us personal data from your synced content in a support request, we use it only to answer that request, with the support sub-processors in Annex 3, and we delete it with the support correspondence, three years after the last message.
  8. Assistance. We give you the information you reasonably need for security, breach notification, data protection impact assessments and consultation with authorities, as far as it concerns the app.
  9. Personal data breaches. We notify you without undue delay, and within 48 hours of becoming aware of a breach affecting your personal data, with the information we have at the time, and we update you as we learn more.
  10. Deletion. After actual uninstallation, Atlassian manages the app's stored data under its Forge storage lifecycle, with a currently documented 28-day retention/recovery window. Reinstalling alone does not restore prior data. Copies soft-deleted by app storage APIs and backups remain subject to Atlassian's retention rules; deletion is not a promise of immediate erasure from every backup. An administrator can also delete the app's data at any time on the settings page: Disconnect asks Google to revoke the app's access and deletes the Google tokens, Use another account then deletes the connection details, rules, links, change positions, recent changes and error messages, and deleting a rule deletes its calendar in your Google account with all its events. Calendars and events in your Google account, and dates the app wrote to Jira issues, remain until separately removed; disconnecting and resetting do not delete them. The app keeps no Atlassian account IDs of its own; JQL that you type into a rule is stored as written.
  11. Demonstrating compliance. We make available the information needed to show that we meet this DPA: this DPA, the privacy policy, and written answers to reasonable security questionnaires. If an audit or inspection beyond that is required by law, you may carry it out, or have an independent auditor carry it out, at your own cost, on at least 30 days' written notice, no more than once a year, without access to other customers' data.
  12. California. Where the California Consumer Privacy Act of 2018, as amended, and its regulations ("CCPA") apply to personal information the app processes for you, EISOL acts as your service provider. We process that personal information only to provide the app to you, as set out in this DPA and the end-user agreement. We do not sell or share it, and we do not retain, use or disclose it for any other purpose or outside our direct business relationship with you. We do not combine it with personal information we receive from others or collect ourselves, except as the CCPA permits. We comply with the CCPA's obligations for service providers and provide the same level of privacy protection the CCPA requires. If we can no longer meet these obligations, we tell you, and you may take reasonable and appropriate steps to stop and remediate unauthorised use. We certify that we understand and will comply with these restrictions.
  13. Google user data. Our use and transfer of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements, as described in the privacy policy.

3. Transfers

EISOL is established in Japan, which the European Commission has recognised as providing an adequate level of protection for personal data (Commission Implementing Decision (EU) 2019/419). The app's processing takes place on Atlassian's platform, in your Jira site and in your Google account, under the terms of those services. EISOL also accesses Marketplace license/contact reports in Japan and can receive shared operational logs and support correspondence, as described in the privacy policy. This DPA covers processing performed by the app on your behalf; it does not describe all of EISOL's separate business records. The provider facts below and the privacy policy distinguish hosting facts from unresolved account-specific and Pages-log disclosures. The privacy policy also describes EISOL's own records: where support email is kept, the AI services EISOL uses to translate and draft support replies, and how long EISOL keeps support and accounting records. No app-wide EEA-only or PINNED assurance is given.

Google's privacy policy says that Google maintains servers around the world and that information may be processed on servers located outside the country where the user lives. For a Google Workspace account, your organisation's agreement with Google also applies. These are provider-wide facts, not a confirmation of where your account's data is kept.

4. General

  • If this DPA and the end-user agreement conflict on the protection of personal data, this DPA prevails.
  • Each party's liability under this DPA is subject to the limitations in the end-user agreement, except where the law does not allow them.
  • This DPA lasts as long as the app processes your personal data. We may update it to reflect changes in the law or in the app; changes that reduce your protection apply only with 30 days' notice on the app's documentation page.

Annex 2 — Security measures

  • The sync code runs on Atlassian Forge and sends the configured issue details between Jira and the connected Google account. EISOL operates no separate sync backend. The current app has no support feature for exporting stored data to EISOL; this does not exclude the Marketplace, log and support information disclosed in the privacy policy.
  • The app connects to Google with OAuth 2.0 and asks only for access to the calendars it makes (calendar.app.created), plus the account's email address and ID. Google tokens are kept in Forge's encrypted secret storage, and the app's client secret in an encrypted Forge variable. No secret is kept in the source code.
  • A connection starts only when a Jira administrator of the site enters the confirmation code shown after the approval in Google.
  • All traffic uses HTTPS.
  • Only Jira administrators can change the app's settings; this is checked against the person's own Jira permissions.
  • Issues with an issue security level are never written to Google.
  • The app keeps fingerprints of what it last wrote, not the event contents.
  • The app writes no issue details and no Google account details to its logs, only what was involved (the calendar name and the issue key) and the kind of error. Site administrators can stop Atlassian from sharing the app's logs with EISOL.
  • The app's dependencies are checked for known vulnerabilities before each release, and vulnerabilities are fixed within the times set by Atlassian's security bug fix policy.
