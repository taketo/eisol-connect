Support and service levels — EISOL Calendar Sync

Last updated: 2026-11-22

How to get help

Raise a request in the EISOL support portal, or email support@eisol.jp, in English or Japanese. Please include your Jira site address and, if you can, the Jira issue key or the name of the calendar concerned.

Response times

RequestFirst response
Critical: the sync has stopped for all calendars, or the app is writing wrong dataWithin 24 hours, every day
Everything else: questions, smaller faults, requestsWithin 5 business days

Business days are Monday to Friday in Japan, excluding Japanese public holidays. We also answer within 24 hours any request that Atlassian identifies as critical.

What support covers

  • Help with installing, connecting and setting up the app.
  • Investigating faults in the app, fixing them, and telling you when the fix is released.
  • Questions about how the app handles your data, including Google user data (see the privacy policy).

It does not cover faults in Jira or Google Calendar themselves, or changes to your own configuration in those products, such as how a calendar is shared, though we will tell you what we find.

Security

Report a security problem to support@eisol.jp with "Security" in the subject. We acknowledge it within 24 hours. We fix critical vulnerabilities within 10 days and high-severity ones within 4 weeks, in line with Atlassian's requirements for Marketplace apps.

How the app is run

The app runs on Atlassian Forge and depends on the availability and limits of Jira, Forge and Google Calendar. Jira changes normally reach Google Calendar within seconds. The app reads changes made in Google every five minutes, and goes over each calendar's issues again once a day. Large calendars, rate limits and temporary failures can delay delivery across several runs. Release notes are in the user guide.

If the app is withdrawn

If we ever stop offering the app, we will keep supporting existing customers for at least one year.
