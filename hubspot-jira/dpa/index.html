Data Processing Addendum — EISOL Field Sync

Last updated: 2026-11-18

This Data Processing Addendum ("DPA") is between EISOL LLC (英智ソリューションズ合同会社), 2-5-1 Sekimae, Musashino-shi, Tokyo 180-0014, Japan ("EISOL", "we") and the organisation that installs EISOL Field Sync ("the app") on its Jira Cloud site ("the customer", "you"). It forms part of the end-user agreement for the app (the Bonterms Standard End User Agreement, Version 1.0, whose Provider-Specific Terms for the app identify this DPA) and applies from the time the app is installed. If you need a signed copy, email support@eisol.jp.

1. Roles

  • You are the controller of the personal data in the content the app syncs. You decide which records, fields, notes and comments are synced, by setting the app's sync rules.
  • EISOL is your processor for the processing the app performs on your behalf. The app performs the configured processing on Atlassian Forge and through your Jira and HubSpot accounts. EISOL is responsible for that app processing; Forge hosting does not transfer that responsibility to Atlassian. The privacy policy separately describes Marketplace reporting, shared operational logs and support correspondence received by EISOL.
  • Annex 1 describes the processing. Words such as "personal data", "processing", "controller", "processor" and "personal data breach" have the meanings given in the GDPR and the UK GDPR.

2. Our commitments

  1. Instructions. The app processes personal data only as set by your sync rules and settings, which are your documented instructions, unless the law requires otherwise; in that case we tell you first, unless the law forbids it. If we believe an instruction breaks data protection law, we tell you.
  2. Confidentiality. Anyone at EISOL who works on the app is bound to keep personal data confidential.
  3. Security. We maintain the measures in Annex 2.
  4. Sub-processors. You authorise the sub-processors in Annex 3. We will announce any new sub-processor on the app's documentation page at least 30 days before it starts. You may object in writing within that period; if we cannot address the objection, you may stop using the app.
  5. Your HubSpot account. The app writes data to the HubSpot account you connect. HubSpot processes that data under your own agreement with HubSpot, and is not our sub-processor.
  6. Requests from individuals. Most requests can be met directly in your Jira site and HubSpot account. If you need more help, we assist you by email. If an individual contacts us directly, we pass the request to you.
  7. Support requests. If you send us personal data from your synced content in a support request, we use it only to answer that request, with the support sub-processors in Annex 3, and we delete it with the support correspondence, three years after the last message.
  8. Assistance. We give you the information you reasonably need for security, breach notification, data protection impact assessments and consultation with authorities, as far as it concerns the app.
  9. Personal data breaches. We notify you without undue delay, and within 48 hours of becoming aware of a breach affecting your personal data, with the information we have at the time, and we update you as we learn more.
  10. Deletion. After actual uninstallation, Atlassian manages the app's stored data under its Forge storage lifecycle, with a currently documented 28-day retention/recovery window. Reinstalling alone does not restore prior data. Copies soft-deleted by app storage APIs and backups remain subject to Atlassian's retention rules; deletion is not a promise of immediate erasure from every backup. An administrator can also delete the app's data at any time on the settings page: Disconnect deletes the HubSpot tokens, Use another account then deletes the links and error messages, and each rule can be deleted. Unlink markers keyed by record ID also remain until manual relinking, reset or installation-data deletion. Jira issues, comments, issue sync properties and HubSpot remote links on Jira issues, and HubSpot records, notes and the three Jira display properties/definitions, remain until separately removed. Reset does not delete those product records. Atlassian account IDs kept in sync rules are reported to Atlassian once a week, and an account that Atlassian reports as closed is removed from the rules.
  11. Demonstrating compliance. We make available the information needed to show that we meet this DPA: this DPA, the privacy policy, and written answers to reasonable security questionnaires. If an audit or inspection beyond that is required by law, you may carry it out, or have an independent auditor carry it out, at your own cost, on at least 30 days' written notice, no more than once a year, without access to other customers' data.
  12. California. Where the California Consumer Privacy Act of 2018, as amended, and its regulations ("CCPA") apply to personal information the app processes for you, EISOL acts as your service provider. We process that personal information only to provide the app to you, as set out in this DPA and the end-user agreement. We do not sell or share it, and we do not retain, use or disclose it for any other purpose or outside our direct business relationship with you. We do not combine it with personal information we receive from others or collect ourselves, except as the CCPA permits. We comply with the CCPA's obligations for service providers and provide the same level of privacy protection the CCPA requires. If we can no longer meet these obligations, we tell you, and you may take reasonable and appropriate steps to stop and remediate unauthorised use. We certify that we understand and will comply with these restrictions.

3. Transfers

EISOL is established in Japan, which the European Commission has recognised as providing an adequate level of protection for personal data (Commission Implementing Decision (EU) 2019/419). The app's processing takes place on Atlassian's platform and in your HubSpot account, under the terms of those services. EISOL also accesses Marketplace license/contact reports in Japan and can receive shared operational logs and support correspondence, as described in the privacy policy. This DPA covers processing performed by the app on your behalf; it does not describe all of EISOL's separate business records. The provider facts below and the privacy policy distinguish hosting candidates from unresolved account-specific and Pages-log disclosures. The privacy policy also describes EISOL's own records: where support email is kept, the AI services EISOL uses to translate and draft support replies, and how long EISOL keeps support and accounting records. No app-wide EEA-only or PINNED assurance is given.

HubSpot's hosting FAQ lists the United States (East and West), Germany (EU), Canada and Australia as its main hosting locations. Your account's assigned location can be checked under Settings → Privacy & Consent → Data Hosting. Its terms, section 5.5.1 and subprocessor list describe processing outside that location, including analytics, support, third-party services and subprocessors. Its migration FAQ also describes pre-migration backups kept in the US East location until migration is complete and deletion is considered safe; it gives no fixed number of days for that deletion. These are provider-wide facts, not a confirmation of your account's actual location or every copy.

4. General

  • If this DPA and the end-user agreement conflict on the protection of personal data, this DPA prevails.
  • Each party's liability under this DPA is subject to the limitations in the end-user agreement, except where the law does not allow them.
  • This DPA lasts as long as the app processes your personal data. We may update it to reflect changes in the law or in the app; changes that reduce your protection apply only with 30 days' notice on the app's documentation page.

Annex 2 — Security measures

  • The sync code runs on Atlassian Forge and sends configured content between Jira and the connected HubSpot account. EISOL operates no separate sync backend. The current app has no support feature for exporting stored sync records to EISOL; this does not exclude the Marketplace, log and support information disclosed in the privacy policy.
  • The app connects to HubSpot with OAuth 2.0. HubSpot tokens are kept in Forge's encrypted secret storage, and the app's client secret in an encrypted Forge variable. No secret is kept in the source code.
  • All traffic uses HTTPS.
  • The app asks only for the permissions the sync needs. Only Jira administrators can change the sync, and only project administrators can link or unlink an issue; both are checked against the person's own Jira permissions.
  • The app keeps fingerprints of the last synced values, not the values themselves.
  • The app writes no synced values to its logs, only what was involved (record IDs, issue keys, property and field names) and the kind of error. Site administrators can stop Atlassian from sharing the app's logs with EISOL.
  • Comments copied into Jira are internal, so Jira Service Management does not send them to customers. Jira comments restricted to a role or group are not copied.
  • The app's dependencies are checked for known vulnerabilities before each release, and vulnerabilities are fixed within the times set by Atlassian's security bug fix policy.
