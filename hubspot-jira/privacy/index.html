Privacy Policy — EISOL Field Sync

Last updated: 2026-11-17

This policy explains what information EISOL Field Sync ("the app") handles and how. The app is made by EISOL LLC (英智ソリューションズ合同会社), 2-5-1 Sekimae, Musashino-shi, Tokyo 180-0014, Japan ("EISOL", "we").

What the app does

The app keeps records in a HubSpot account (tickets, deals and companies, and the notes on them) in step with issues and comments in a Jira Cloud site, in both directions. A Jira administrator connects one HubSpot account to the Jira site and chooses which records and fields are synced.

Where the app runs

The app runs entirely on Atlassian Forge, Atlassian's platform for apps. EISOL runs no servers for the app. During routine sync, mapped content moves between Forge, Jira and the connected HubSpot account. The app does not send that content to a separate EISOL-operated server. Marketplace reporting, shared operational logs and support correspondence are separate information flows described below.

Information the app handles

  • Content you choose to sync. The HubSpot properties and Jira fields named in the app's sync rules (for example a ticket's subject and description, a deal's close date, a priority), and the notes and comments on synced records. The app reads them from one side and writes them to the other. To find what changed, the app also reads, without keeping them, the mapped properties of other changed records of the types you sync, and the notes changed in your HubSpot account; it copies only those on synced records. To resolve competing edits, it temporarily reads change histories for the mapped fields to establish when those fields changed.
  • Links between records. For each synced pair: the HubSpot record ID, the Jira issue ID and key, fingerprints of the last synced values (short one-way hashes, not the values themselves), and when the pair was last synced. The same for each copied note and comment. Retry decisions and unfinished history scans keep identifiers, timestamps, fingerprints and page positions, not the historical field values. Unlinking a record leaves a marker keyed by its record ID to prevent automatic re-creation.
  • Connection details. The HubSpot account ID, its time zone and web address, and the access and refresh tokens HubSpot issues to the app. Tokens are kept in Forge's encrypted secret storage.
  • Settings. The sync rules the administrator sets.
  • Error messages. Short descriptions of failed sync steps. The app keeps the last 30, and its settings page shows the five most recent to Jira administrators. They name records by ID. When HubSpot or Jira refuses a change, the message includes the reason they gave (up to 500 characters), which can repeat part of the refused value. These messages stay in the app's storage; the log lines Atlassian shares with EISOL never include them.

Marketplace and support information. For paid apps, Atlassian makes license and transaction reports available to EISOL. They can include license or entitlement IDs, status, tier and dates; the customer organisation, site and country; and billing or technical contacts (name, email and, where provided, postal address and phone). EISOL accesses these reports from Japan for licensing and service administration. We also receive correspondence and files you choose to send us for support. These records are separate from the synced content.

The app does not collect card details, use cookies or track people. Atlassian handles Marketplace payments.

Where it is stored

The links, connection details, settings, retry metadata and errors are kept in Forge hosted storage. Atlassian's Forge location documentation lists Australia, Canada, Germany and Ireland (EU), India, Japan, Singapore, South Korea, Switzerland, the United Kingdom and the United States, plus a Global option whose realms Atlassian determines. Forge-hosted storage follows the host Jira site's pinned location and migration where applicable. Forge execution can sometimes occur in another location; storage pinning is not a promise that every processing step stays in that location.

Synced content is also stored as Jira issues and HubSpot records in those services' hosting locations.

HubSpot's hosting FAQ lists the United States (East and West), Germany (EU), Canada and Australia as its main hosting locations. Your account's assigned location can be checked under Settings → Privacy & Consent → Data Hosting. Its terms, section 5.5.1 and subprocessor list describe processing outside that location, including analytics, support, third-party services and subprocessors. Its migration FAQ also describes pre-migration backups kept in the US East location until migration is complete and deletion is considered safe; it gives no fixed number of days for that deletion. These are provider-wide facts, not a confirmation of your account's actual location or every copy.

This app does not provide an app-wide residency or PINNED guarantee: HubSpot storage and the GitHub-hosted connection page are outside Forge's storage controls. Marketplace reports remain on Atlassian's reporting service and are accessible to EISOL in Japan.

EISOL's own records. EISOL keeps support email with its email providers: SAKURA internet Inc. in Japan, and Google LLC (Gmail), which stores email in data centres in several countries, including the United States. To read messages in English and to draft replies, EISOL gives the text needed for a reply to AI services (currently Anthropic, PBC and OpenAI OpCo, LLC), with settings under which they do not use it to train their models; they may process it in the United States and other countries. EISOL accesses Marketplace reports on Atlassian's reporting service from Japan and keeps the copies it needs for its accounts on its own computers in Japan.

Who can see it

  • Your Jira users and your HubSpot users, according to the permissions in each product.
  • Atlassian, as the operator of Forge, under Atlassian's own terms and privacy policy.
  • HubSpot, as the operator of your HubSpot account, under your agreement with HubSpot and HubSpot's privacy policy.
  • EISOL develops and operates the app and is responsible for its processing of personal data under this policy; Atlassian is not responsible for EISOL's processing. The current app has no support feature for sending stored sync records or mapped field values to EISOL. This is not a claim that EISOL receives no customer information: we can see Marketplace reports, support messages and the shared operational logs described here. When the app is installed, Atlassian shares the app's logs with EISOL by default, so that we can find faults; a site administrator can turn this off at admin.atlassian.com. The app writes no synced content to its logs, only what was involved (record IDs, issue keys, property and field names) and the kind of error.

Connecting to HubSpot

When an administrator connects HubSpot, HubSpot sends the browser to a page EISOL hosts on GitHub Pages (https://connect.eisol.jp/hubspot/), with a one-time authorization code and connection state in the page's address. The page forwards those values to the app on Atlassian's platform; its own code does not persist them. The authorization code can be used only once, and only together with the app's client secret, which is not on that page and is never sent to GitHub. The app then keeps the approval waiting, and connects the account only when a Jira administrator of your site enters the confirmation code shown after the approval. This does not determine the hosting provider's logging or retention.

GitHub Pages documents that it logs and stores visitors' IP addresses for security, whether or not they are signed in. GitHub's default US storage description for GitHub.com is general: it does not establish that every Pages delivery copy or access log is stored only in the US. Its privacy statement also describes storage and processing in other countries. The full countries of storage for this Pages site's logs, whether and how its code/state query values are logged, and their fixed retention period remain unconfirmed. EISOL cannot see or change these logs.

How long it is kept

  • Links and sync metadata are kept while needed for the connected account. Unlinking replaces a record link with a marker keyed by its HubSpot record ID; that marker remains until the record is manually linked again, an administrator completes Use another account, or the installation data is removed. It prevents the next poll from creating the issue again.
  • When an administrator disconnects HubSpot, the app revokes the site's access to the HubSpot account and deletes its tokens. The links are kept until the administrator selects Use another account, and the rules until the administrator deletes them, so the same account can be connected again. To remove the app from the HubSpot account itself, uninstall it in HubSpot.
  • After actual uninstallation from Jira, Atlassian manages the app's stored data under its Forge storage lifecycle. Atlassian currently documents a 28-day retention/recovery window; reinstallation alone does not restore the old data. This does not promise immediate erasure of all backups. Deletion through the app removes primary access; Atlassian's retention rules also apply to soft-deleted copies.
  • Content already written to Jira or HubSpot stays there until you delete it: issues, comments, notes, field values, the issue's sync property and the HubSpot record link added to the Jira issue (remote link). The three Jira display properties and their definitions in HubSpot are not removed automatically. Disconnect and Use another account do not clean up these product records. Marketplace and support records are separate from Forge installation data; uninstalling the app does not erase them.
  • EISOL deletes support correspondence three years after the last message in a conversation. It keeps Marketplace reports and its other accounting records for seven years, the period Japanese tax law generally requires, and then deletes them.

Data protection roles

For personal data in synced content, the customer (the organisation that uses Jira and HubSpot) decides what is synced and is the controller, and EISOL is the customer's processor for the processing the app performs. The app performs that processing on Forge and through the customer's Jira and HubSpot accounts. Operational logs, Marketplace reports and support correspondence are handled as described above. Our Data Processing Addendum forms part of the app's end-user agreement.

Your rights

To ask about the information the app handles, or to exercise your rights under data protection laws, contact us. Requests about synced content can usually be met in your Jira site and HubSpot account, for example by deleting a record or comment. Contact EISOL about Marketplace contact records or correspondence held by EISOL.

Security

The app connects to HubSpot with OAuth and never sees HubSpot passwords. Tokens are kept in encrypted storage, and all traffic uses HTTPS. Only Jira administrators can change the sync, and only project administrators can link an issue to a HubSpot record or unlink it. Comments the app copies into Jira are internal, so on Jira Service Management they are not sent to customers, and Jira comments restricted to a role or group are not copied to HubSpot.
