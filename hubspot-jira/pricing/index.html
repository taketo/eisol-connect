Pricing — EISOL Field Sync

Last updated: 2026-11-17

EISOL Field Sync is sold through Atlassian Marketplace, and Atlassian bills it with your Jira subscription, monthly or annually. As for every Jira Cloud app, the price depends on the number of users on your Jira site, not on how many of them use the app. Prices on this page are in US dollars.

Up to 10 users

Jira sites with up to 10 users pay a flat USD 10 a month, or USD 100 a year. You can try the app free of charge before you buy, with the Marketplace's free trial.

Monthly subscriptions

Sites with up to 10 users pay a flat USD 10 a month. On a site with 11 or more users, every user is counted, and each band of users has its own price:

What applies

The prices on the Atlassian Marketplace listing and the total at Atlassian's checkout, including any tax, are the ones that apply. Atlassian's terms for Marketplace purchases, renewals and refunds apply; this page adds no terms of its own.
