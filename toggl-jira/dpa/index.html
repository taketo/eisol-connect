Data Processing Addendum — EISOL Time Sync

Last updated: 2026-11-22

This Data Processing Addendum ("DPA") is between EISOL LLC (英智ソリューションズ合同会社), 2-5-1 Sekimae, Musashino-shi, Tokyo 180-0014, Japan ("EISOL", "we") and the organisation that installs EISOL Time Sync ("the app") on its Jira Cloud site ("the customer", "you"). It forms part of the end-user agreement for the app (the Bonterms Standard End User Agreement, Version 1.0, whose Provider-Specific Terms for the app identify this DPA) and applies from the time the app is installed. If you need a signed copy, email support@eisol.jp.

1. Roles

  • You are the controller of the personal data in the time the app copies. You decide which Toggl workspace is connected, which people are linked and which issues receive their time.
  • EISOL is your processor for the processing the app performs on your behalf. The app performs the configured processing on Atlassian Forge and through your Jira site and Toggl workspace. EISOL is responsible for that app processing; Forge hosting does not transfer that responsibility to Atlassian. The privacy policy separately describes Marketplace reporting, shared operational logs and support correspondence received by EISOL.
  • Annex 1 describes the processing. Words such as "personal data", "processing", "controller", "processor" and "personal data breach" have the meanings given in the GDPR and the UK GDPR.

2. Our commitments

  1. Instructions. The app processes personal data only as set by the app's settings and each person's confirmation and choices, which are your documented instructions, unless the law requires otherwise; in that case we tell you first, unless the law forbids it. If we believe an instruction breaks data protection law, we tell you.
  2. Confidentiality. Anyone at EISOL who works on the app is bound to keep personal data confidential.
  3. Security. We maintain the measures in Annex 2.
  4. Sub-processors. You authorise the sub-processors in Annex 3. We will announce any new sub-processor on the app's documentation page at least 30 days before it starts. You may object in writing within that period; if we cannot address the objection, you may stop using the app.
  5. Your Toggl workspace. The app reads data from the Toggl workspace you connect, and writes nothing to it. Toggl processes that data under your own agreement with Toggl, and is not our sub-processor.
  6. Requests from individuals. Most requests can be met directly in your Jira site and Toggl workspace, and a person can stop the copying of their own time on their page in the app. If you need more help, we assist you by email. If an individual contacts us directly, we pass the request to you.
  7. Support requests. If you send us personal data from your copied time in a support request, we use it only to answer that request, with the support sub-processors in Annex 3, and we delete it with the support correspondence, three years after the last message.
  8. Assistance. We give you the information you reasonably need for security, breach notification, data protection impact assessments and consultation with authorities, as far as it concerns the app.
  9. Personal data breaches. We notify you without undue delay, and within 48 hours of becoming aware of a breach affecting your personal data, with the information we have at the time, and we update you as we learn more.
  10. Deletion. After actual uninstallation, Atlassian manages the app's stored data under its Forge storage lifecycle, with a currently documented 28-day retention/recovery window. Reinstalling alone does not restore prior data. Copies soft-deleted by app storage APIs and backups remain subject to Atlassian's retention rules; deletion is not a promise of immediate erasure from every backup. An administrator can also delete the app's data at any time on the settings page: Disconnect deletes the Toggl API key, and Reset the app deletes the key, the people, the rules, the links, the choices, the recent copies and the error messages. Worklogs written to Jira remain until separately removed; a reset does not delete them. The Atlassian account IDs the app keeps are reported to Atlassian once a week, and an account that Atlassian reports as closed is erased from the app's storage.
  11. Demonstrating compliance. We make available the information needed to show that we meet this DPA: this DPA, the privacy policy, and written answers to reasonable security questionnaires. If an audit or inspection beyond that is required by law, you may carry it out, or have an independent auditor carry it out, at your own cost, on at least 30 days' written notice, no more than once a year, without access to other customers' data.
  12. California. Where the California Consumer Privacy Act of 2018, as amended, and its regulations ("CCPA") apply to personal information the app processes for you, EISOL acts as your service provider. We process that personal information only to provide the app to you, as set out in this DPA and the end-user agreement. We do not sell or share it, and we do not retain, use or disclose it for any other purpose or outside our direct business relationship with you. We do not combine it with personal information we receive from others or collect ourselves, except as the CCPA permits. We comply with the CCPA's obligations for service providers and provide the same level of privacy protection the CCPA requires. If we can no longer meet these obligations, we tell you, and you may take reasonable and appropriate steps to stop and remediate unauthorised use. We certify that we understand and will comply with these restrictions.

3. Transfers

EISOL is established in Japan, which the European Commission has recognised as providing an adequate level of protection for personal data (Commission Implementing Decision (EU) 2019/419). The app's processing takes place on Atlassian's platform and through your Toggl workspace, under the terms of those services. EISOL also accesses Marketplace license/contact reports in Japan and can receive shared operational logs and support correspondence, as described in the privacy policy. This DPA covers processing performed by the app on your behalf; it does not describe all of EISOL's separate business records. The privacy policy also describes EISOL's own records: where support email is kept, the AI services EISOL uses to translate and draft support replies, and how long EISOL keeps support and accounting records. No app-wide EEA-only or PINNED assurance is given.

Where Toggl stores and processes your workspace's data is set by your agreement with Toggl and Toggl's own documents; the app does not change it.

4. General

  • If this DPA and the end-user agreement conflict on the protection of personal data, this DPA prevails.
  • Each party's liability under this DPA is subject to the limitations in the end-user agreement, except where the law does not allow them.
  • This DPA lasts as long as the app processes your personal data. We may update it to reflect changes in the law or in the app; changes that reduce your protection apply only with 30 days' notice on the app's documentation page.

Annex 2 — Security measures

  • The app runs on Atlassian Forge and copies time entries from Toggl into Jira. EISOL operates no separate backend. The current app has no support feature for exporting stored records to EISOL; this does not exclude the Marketplace, log and support information disclosed in the privacy policy.
  • The Toggl API key is kept in Forge's encrypted secret storage. It is sent only to Toggl's API, in the request header, and is never shown again, logged or written to error messages. No secret is kept in the source code.
  • All traffic uses HTTPS.
  • The app asks only for the permissions it needs. Only Jira administrators can connect Toggl, link people or change rules; this is checked against the person's own Jira permissions.
  • Time is written in a person's name only after that person has confirmed, on their own page, that the Toggl person linked to them is them, and only while they do. A person can choose issues only for their own entries, can find only issues they can see, and can choose only issues they can log work on.
  • The app changes and deletes only worklogs it created, which it marks with a worklog property, and only while the person confirms the link. It deletes the worklog of an entry that seems deleted in Toggl only when two checks in a row miss the entry, and after reading all of that person's entries around that day again; when many entries seem gone at once, it deletes nothing.
  • The app keeps fingerprints of what it wrote, not the descriptions of time entries.
  • The app writes no time entries to its logs: only what was involved in an error (entry IDs, issue keys) and its kind, and once a week the number of account IDs reported to Atlassian. Site administrators can stop Atlassian from sharing the app's logs with EISOL.
  • The app's dependencies are checked for known vulnerabilities before each release, and vulnerabilities are fixed within the times set by Atlassian's security bug fix policy.
